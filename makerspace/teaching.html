Teaching in the Makerspace

For each method of manufacture in the Hello Maker Studio, users must complete a combination of online training modules and in-person certifications. These trainings emphasize safety, proper operation, and independent use of equipment.

Most tools are supported through online training, with staff available to assist users during first-time use or unfamiliar processes.

Writing Online Training Modules

I contribute to the development of online training modules used in the makerspace. These modules are designed to function both as an initial introduction and as a comprehensive reference during active use.

In-Person Trainings

I regularly conduct in-person trainings for 3D printers and laser cutters, and have helped expand internal CNC training capabilities.

CNC Machining

I was responsible for setup, testing, and creating training materials for a new desktop CNC machine introduced to the makerspace.
